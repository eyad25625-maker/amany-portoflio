About Me:

I am an art teacher and art supervisor at Modern Narmer Language School in Giza, where I have worked since 2001. I teach art to every grade, and for the last five years I have supervised the art programme.

My work combines curriculum development, assessment and age-appropriate activities with hands-on painting, paper, foam and fabric construction. I also plan, design and build the school’s event installations, from national commemorations to Ramadan, Back to School, Children’s Day, Christmas and New Year.

Statement

This portfolio documents the installations I design and build for the school’s annual events. Each piece is hand-painted or hand-cut from foam, fabric, foil and paper, and made at a scale students and families can step into.
